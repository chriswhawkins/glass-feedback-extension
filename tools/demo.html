A little context.

What works here, and what needs the extension?

Drawing, arrows, rectangles, element outlines, notes, colors, and opacity use the same UI source as the extension. Camera lets you try choosing and adjusting a region, but the demo cannot read other tabs, export screenshots, or copy page images. It explains that limitation instead of pretending to save an image.

How do I get my page back?

Press Esc, or choose Browse above. Your annotations stay visible, but the tool stops intercepting page gestures. Choose a demo tool to continue. In Chrome, the browser's extension icon turns Glass Feedback off across supported tabs.

Does my feedback go anywhere?

The tools process annotations locally. This demo saves tool preferences in this browser's local storage; notes and drawings last only until reload or navigation. There are no analytics or annotation uploads in this page. Choose Copy or Save in the extension when you want an image to share yourself.

Will it work on every website?

Chrome blocks extensions on protected pages such as the Chrome Web Store and chrome:// pages. Full-page capture scrolls and stitches the page; sticky elements can repeat and virtualized content may be incomplete. A region capture is often the simpler choice.
